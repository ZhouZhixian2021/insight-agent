# Product report delivery disclosures

English | [中文](guide.zh.md)

## Change

Product report delivery no longer treats a nonempty operation-failure list, limited search coverage, unanswered questions, or missing independent corroboration as standalone draft reasons. These facts remain disclosed in the report. A final report does not imply complete coverage or independent validation. Invalid citation identities, cancellation, and report-generation failures remain checked. No verified evidence still blocks report creation.

New investigations also skip independent comparison calls and corroboration-only searches. Unanswered-question gap searches and original-source support review remain active. The progress card has four stages; saved historical comparisons remain inspectable.

Missing or different page publisher declarations and historical uncertain or conflicting comparisons are disclosures, not delivery blockers. Source-supported material remains available to report synthesis; differing claims retain their conditions and uncertainty.

Source eligibility, snapshot mismatches, failed or changed source revalidation, and the absence of any supported claim no longer independently prevent final delivery. Scope-qualified observations retain their limitations. Unmatched snapshots and unsupported statements remain inspectable but are not presented as confirmed conclusions. Resumption retains the saved source snapshots and records fresh observations separately; it no longer discards prior evidence because a live page changed.

## Migration

1. Rebuild and restart the Web service before creating a new investigation.
2. Read the report's question-level limitations and search/acquisition disclosure even when delivery is final.
3. Retain saved reports as historical results: this change does not rewrite their status. Verify the policy with a new approved investigation or an explicit offline replay.

Review judgments are admitted individually; missing or invalid judgments do not discard valid siblings or independently block delivery. Missing records are disclosed per question. Reviews may associate evidence with another approved question while preserving extraction-time associations. Synthesis places evidence markers after supported sentences; validated references become numbered footnotes and clickable superscripts in chat and HTML. Existing paragraph-end citations are not relocated automatically; regenerate those reports.
