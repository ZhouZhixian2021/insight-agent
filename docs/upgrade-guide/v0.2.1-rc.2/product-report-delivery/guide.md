# Product report delivery disclosures

English | [中文](guide.zh.md)

## Change

Product report delivery no longer treats a nonempty operation-failure list, limited search coverage, unanswered questions, or missing independent corroboration as standalone draft reasons. These facts remain disclosed in the report. A final report does not imply complete coverage or independent validation. Invalid citations, ineligible or stale sources, missing semantic reviews, cancellation, and report-generation failures remain checked. No verified evidence still blocks report creation.

New investigations also skip independent comparison calls and corroboration-only searches. Unanswered-question gap searches and original-source support review remain active. The progress card has four stages; saved historical comparisons remain inspectable.

Missing or different page publisher declarations and historical uncertain or conflicting comparisons are disclosures, not delivery blockers. Source-supported material remains available to report synthesis; differing claims retain their conditions and uncertainty.

## Migration

1. Rebuild and restart the Web service before creating a new investigation.
2. Read the report's question-level limitations and search/acquisition disclosure even when delivery is final.
3. Retain saved reports as historical results: this change does not rewrite their status. Verify the policy with a new approved investigation or an explicit offline replay.
