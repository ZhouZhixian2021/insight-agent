# Academic insight report format

Use this hierarchy as a report contract, not as a fixed section count. Omit a subsection only when it has no evidence, and name the resulting coverage limit in the methodology.

## Opening pages

1. Title, technology scope, publication window, and report date.
2. Research scope: sources, inclusion rules, publication-status policy, and evidence limitations.
3. Executive insight: the central change in the field, the strongest supporting signals, and the main uncertainty.
4. Direction map: a compact table of research layers, directions, and current concerns.

## Each research direction

1. Direction title expressed as a change, tension, or research question.
2. Direction overview explaining why the problem matters.
3. Current concerns presented as two to four evidence-backed themes.
4. Representative work with title, authors, venue or repository, date, publication status, and stable link.
5. Core mechanism. Include a figure only when its source and caption can be verified.
6. Key result. Report numerical comparisons only from inspected full text, tables, or figures and retain metric conditions.
7. Direction judgment separating observed evidence from synthesis.
8. Engineering or application implication when it follows from the evidence and serves the requested audience.

## Synthesis and references

1. Cross-direction synthesis: shared state, dependencies, contradictions, and emerging system structure.
2. Open problems and evidence gaps.
3. Conclusion answering the research questions without introducing new claims.
4. Numbered bibliography with complete titles and stable DOI, proceedings, repository, or publisher links.

Use clear tables for direction maps and comparable experimental results. Do not force incomparable metrics, datasets, or evaluation settings into one ranking.
